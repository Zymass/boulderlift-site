Privacy Policy

BoulderLift · Last updated June 2026

BoulderLift is built to keep your data on your device. We do not run servers and we do not collect, sell, or share personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. BoulderLift does not collect any personal data. Your scans, machine profiles, saved boulders, and settings live only on your iPhone (and your private iCloud backup, if you use one).

Camera

BoulderLift uses the camera and LiDAR sensor only to scan a rock and measure its size. Camera frames and the 3D mesh are processed on your device in real time and are never uploaded or stored off-device.

Network

The app works fully offline. The only network connection it makes is to RevenueCat, our purchase provider, to process and restore the one-time Pro unlock. That request contains a random anonymous app-user identifier and your purchase receipt — not your name, email, location, or scans. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Tracking & analytics

None. There is no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking across apps or websites.

Children

BoulderLift is a professional tool rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
